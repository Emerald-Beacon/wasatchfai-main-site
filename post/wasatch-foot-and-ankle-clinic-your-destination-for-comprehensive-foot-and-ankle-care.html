Wasatch Foot & Ankle Clinic: Your Destination for Comprehensive Foot & Ankle Care

From bunion surgery to diabetic foot exams, here's what "comprehensive care" actually looks like at our Farmington and South Ogden offices.

By the physicians at Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute · Updated July 13, 2026

Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute provides comprehensive foot and ankle care for patients throughout Northern Utah — from routine nail and callus care to complex reconstructive surgery. Our board-certified, fellowship-trained podiatrists treat the full range of foot and ankle conditions at two convenient locations, Farmington and South Ogden, combining advanced technology with a personalized approach to every treatment plan.

Choosing a foot and ankle specialist is about more than finding someone nearby — it's about finding a team that can handle whatever your feet throw at you, from a nagging bunion to a sports injury to long-term diabetic foot management. Here's an overview of the care we provide and how we approach each patient's treatment.

What services does Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute offer?

We treat patients across every stage of foot and ankle health, whether that means resolving an acute injury, managing a chronic condition, or simply keeping feet healthy through routine care. Our core service areas include:

  • Foot and ankle surgery for bunions, hammertoes, heel spurs, fractures, and other structural problems that haven't responded to conservative care
  • Custom orthotics designed around your specific foot shape and gait to relieve pain and improve function — see our custom orthotics page
  • Diabetic foot care, including regular exams, neuropathy screening, and wound treatment to prevent complications
  • Sports medicine for the sprains, stress fractures, tendon injuries, and overuse conditions that come with an active Utah lifestyle
  • Routine foot care, including nail care, callus management, and general maintenance for healthy feet

What makes our approach to care different?

We built our practice around a simple idea: every patient's feet — and every patient's goals — are different, so treatment shouldn't be one-size-fits-all. A few things you can expect when you visit us:

Experienced, fellowship-trained physicians

Our podiatrists bring years of specialized training and hands-on experience to every case, from the most common foot complaints to complex surgical reconstructions. You're seeing physicians who focus exclusively on the foot and ankle.

Personalized treatment plans

We don't jump straight to surgery. Every plan starts with a thorough evaluation, and most conditions improve with conservative measures like bracing, orthotics, physical therapy, or in-office procedures before surgery is ever discussed.

Advanced technology

From in-office diagnostic imaging to minimally invasive procedures like the Tenex procedure and extracorporeal shockwave therapy, we use modern tools to diagnose accurately and treat efficiently — often avoiding open surgery altogether.

Compassionate, patient-first care

Foot and ankle pain affects everything from your mood to your ability to work and exercise. We take time to listen, explain your options clearly, and make sure you understand the "why" behind every recommendation.

Who do we treat?

Our patients range from student athletes recovering from a sprained ankle to older adults managing diabetic foot complications, and everyone in between. Common reasons patients visit us include:

  • Persistent heel, arch, or ball-of-foot pain
  • Bunions, hammertoes, or other toe deformities
  • Ankle sprains, fractures, or chronic instability
  • Diabetic foot changes, numbness, or slow-healing wounds
  • Ingrown toenails, fungal infections, or other nail concerns
  • Sports injuries and overuse conditions

Why choose Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute?

Foot and ankle problems rarely improve on their own, and the longer you wait, the more they can affect the way you walk, stand, and move through your day. Our goal is to get you an accurate diagnosis quickly and get you started on a treatment plan that fits your life — not just your X-ray.

With offices in Farmington and South Ogden, same-day appointments for urgent issues, and a team that treats the full spectrum of foot and ankle conditions, we aim to be the only foot and ankle practice you need. When you're ready, you can request an appointment or explore our full list of services.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What conditions does Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute treat?

We treat the full range of foot and ankle conditions, including bunions, hammertoes, heel pain, ankle sprains and fractures, diabetic foot complications, ingrown nails, fungal infections, and sports injuries. If it involves the foot or ankle, our team can likely help.

Do I need a referral to see a podiatrist?

Most patients do not need a referral to schedule with us, though this can depend on your specific insurance plan. Our office can help verify your benefits and let you know if a referral is required before your visit.

Does the clinic offer same-day appointments?

Yes. Same-day appointments are available for urgent issues such as acute injuries, severe pain, or suspected fractures. For non-urgent concerns, we recommend scheduling ahead to find a time that works for you.

Will I need surgery for my foot or ankle problem?

Not usually. Most foot and ankle conditions improve with conservative treatment such as bracing, orthotics, physical therapy, or minimally invasive in-office procedures. Surgery is generally reserved for cases that do not respond to nonsurgical care.

Where are your offices located?

We have two Northern Utah locations: Farmington at 473 W. Bourne Circle, Suite 2, and South Ogden at 945 Chambers Street, Suite 3. Both offices offer the same full range of services.

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can request an appointment online, or call our Farmington office at 801-451-7500 or our South Ogden office at 801-627-2122.
