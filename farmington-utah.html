Keeping Farmington active and on its feet

Keeping you active and on your feet — that's our first priority at Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute. Whether it's the slightest discomfort or pain that just won't quit, our Farmington team is here to identify the source and recommend the best treatment plan for the best possible outcome.

This page is built with the belief that the more you understand about your foot health, the better we're able to serve you and your feet. From routine check-ups to advanced procedures, our Farmington clinic combines big-city expertise with the warmth of a team that knows your name.

What to expect at your visit

When you arrive, our front desk team will get you checked in quickly so you can spend less time in the waiting room and more time with your doctor. Every visit starts with a conversation — we listen first, examine thoroughly, and explain your options in plain English before recommending a plan.

Many common conditions can be treated the same day with comfortable, in-office procedures, so you can often walk out feeling better than you walked in.

Same-day emergencies welcome

Call us for same-day emergencies — ingrown toenails, sprains, breaks, and more. If you're dealing with sudden foot or ankle pain, don't wait; reach out and we'll get you seen.
