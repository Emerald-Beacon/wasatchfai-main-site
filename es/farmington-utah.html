Nuestra clínica de Farmington atiende a las comunidades del condado de Davis desde 473 W. Bourne Circle, Suite 2, muy cerca de Park Lane y de Station Park. El teléfono directo es el 801-451-7500 y contamos con personal que habla español.

Podiatrist serving Farmington & Davis County

Our Farmington clinic is a full-service foot and ankle practice serving Farmington and the surrounding Davis County communities. We care for patients from Kaysville, Centerville, Fruit Heights, Layton, Bountiful, and across Northern Utah — treating everyday problems like heel pain and ingrown toenails as well as sports injuries, diabetic foot care, and reconstructive surgery. Keeping you active and on your feet is our first priority.

We built this practice on a simple belief: the more you understand about your foot health, the better we can serve you. From routine check-ups to advanced, minimally invasive procedures, our Farmington team combines big-city expertise with the warmth of a clinic that knows your name.

What to expect at your visit

Every visit starts with a conversation. Our front desk team checks you in quickly so you spend less time in the waiting room and more time with your doctor, who will listen first, examine thoroughly, and explain your options in plain English before recommending a plan. Many common conditions can be treated the same day with comfortable, in-office procedures, so you can often walk out feeling better than you walked in.

To make your first visit smooth, bring your photo ID, insurance card, a list of current medications, and any imaging or records related to your foot or ankle problem. Wearing or bringing the shoes you spend the most time in also helps us assess your gait and footwear. New patients can save time by completing our patient intake forms before arriving.

Directions & parking

You’ll find us at 473 W. Bourne Circle, Suite 2, in Farmington, just off Park Lane near the Station Park shopping district and easily reached from I-15 (Exit 325) and the Legacy Parkway. Free surface parking is available directly outside the building, and the entrance and exam rooms are on ground level for easy access. The FrontRunner Farmington Station is only a few minutes away for patients traveling by transit.

Same-day emergencies welcome

Call us for same-day foot and ankle emergencies — ingrown toenails, sprains, fractures, wounds, and sudden severe pain. If you’re dealing with an urgent problem, don’t wait; call 801-451-7500 and we’ll do our best to get you seen the same day.

Conditions we treat in Farmington

Our podiatrists diagnose and treat the full range of foot and ankle conditions under one roof, so most patients can complete their care without a referral elsewhere. Commonly treated conditions include:

  • Plantar fasciitis and chronic heel pain
  • Bunions, including Lapiplasty 3D bunion correction
  • Ingrown toenails and nail problems
  • Ankle fractures, sprains, and ankle instability
  • Achilles tendonitis and tendon injuries
  • Flat feet and custom orthotics
  • Diabetic foot care and limb salvage

Browse our full list of services to see everything we offer, from conservative care to advanced surgical options.

Insurance & payment

We accept nearly all major insurance carriers, including United Healthcare, Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, PEHP, Tricare, and Aetna. If you’re unsure whether we’re in-network with your plan, call our office and our team will help verify your coverage before your visit. You can also pay a bill online through our secure InstaMed portal.
