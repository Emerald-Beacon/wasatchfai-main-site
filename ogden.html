Foot & ankle care built for Ogden's active community

Ogden sits at the mouth of some of Utah's best canyons, and its residents put their feet to work — hiking the Bonneville Shoreline Trail, mountain biking above 29th Street, skiing at nearby resorts, and running the Ogden River Parkway. That lifestyle is wonderful for overall health but hard on feet and ankles. Our South Ogden clinic, a short drive from downtown Ogden and Weber State University, is built to get active people diagnosed and moving again quickly.

We're not a walk-in urgent care and we're not a big impersonal hospital system — we're a dedicated podiatry practice where the same doctors see you visit after visit. Whether you're dealing with a nagging overuse injury or need a same-day evaluation after a fall, our Ogden-area patients get direct access to board-certified, fellowship-trained podiatrists.

Common reasons Ogden patients visit us

Because of the terrain and climate around Ogden, certain conditions show up often in our exam rooms. Trail runners and hikers frequently come in with plantar fasciitis and Achilles tightness from uneven terrain. Winter brings its own hazards — ice-related ankle sprains and fractures spike every year. And, like anywhere, we see plenty of bunions, ingrown toenails, and diabetic foot concerns that need ongoing management.

  • Plantar fasciitis and heel pain from trail running or standing jobs
  • Ankle fractures and sprains from winter falls or sports
  • Achilles tendonitis in cyclists and runners
  • Bunions and hallux rigidus
  • Diabetic foot care and wound management

Getting to our clinic from Ogden

From downtown Ogden or Weber State, our South Ogden office at 945 Chambers Street, Suite 3 is typically a 10-15 minute drive via Harrison Boulevard or Washington Boulevard. Free parking is available right outside the building, and the entrance is at ground level — no stairs to navigate on a sprained ankle or in a walking boot.

Same-day appointments for sudden injuries

Rolled an ankle on the trail or woke up with sudden, severe foot pain? Call 801-627-2122 as early as you can — we reserve time each day for foot and ankle emergencies, including fractures, deep lacerations, infected ingrown toenails, and acute sports injuries.

Insurance accepted

We're in-network with most major insurance carriers, including United Healthcare, Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, PEHP, Tricare, and Aetna. Our front office can verify your specific benefits before your visit — just give us a call.

Common Questions

Ogden-area patient FAQs

Is there a podiatrist office in Ogden, Utah?

Our nearest full clinic to Ogden is in South Ogden at 945 Chambers Street, Suite 3 — about a 10-15 minute drive from downtown Ogden via Harrison or Washington Boulevard.

Do you treat sports and trail-related foot injuries?

Yes. We regularly treat hikers, trail runners, and cyclists from the Ogden foothills and canyons for issues like plantar fasciitis, Achilles tendonitis, ankle sprains, and stress-related foot pain.

Can I get a same-day appointment for a foot or ankle injury?

We reserve time each day for foot and ankle emergencies. Call 801-627-2122 as early as possible and we'll do our best to work you in the same day.

Is parking available at the clinic?

Yes, free surface parking is available directly outside the building, with a ground-level entrance — convenient if you're in a walking boot or on crutches.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept nearly all major carriers, including United Healthcare, Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, PEHP, Tricare, and Aetna. Call ahead and our team will verify your specific benefits.

Do I need a referral to see a podiatrist here?

Most patients can book directly without a referral, though some insurance plans require one. You can request an appointment online or call to check.
