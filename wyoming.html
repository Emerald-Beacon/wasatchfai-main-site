Why Wyoming patients choose Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute

Southwestern Wyoming has limited access to fellowship-trained podiatric surgeons, so many residents already travel to the Wasatch Front for specialty medical care. We see patients regularly from Evanston, Mountain View, Lyman, Kemmerer, Rock Springs, and Green River who prefer the shorter drive to our Farmington or South Ogden clinics over farther options. Both offices offer the same board-certified physicians, the same conservative-first treatment philosophy, and the same same-day availability for emergencies.

Whether you need a single consultation, ongoing diabetic foot care, or a surgical procedure with local follow-up visits, our team is set up to make travel from Wyoming as painless as possible — including coordinating appointment times that minimize the number of trips required.

Getting here from Wyoming

From Evanston and the Bridger Valley, I-80 west to I-84 connects directly to Farmington and South Ogden, typically a 60-90 minute drive depending on your starting point. Patients from Kemmerer, Rock Springs, and Green River generally take I-80 to I-84 as well, arriving in roughly two to three hours. Both clinics have free parking directly outside the building with ground-level entrances.

Conditions we treat

We treat the full spectrum of foot and ankle conditions for patients of all ages, including:

  • Plantar fasciitis and heel pain
  • Bunions and Lapiplasty 3D bunion correction
  • Ankle fractures and ankle instability
  • Diabetic foot care and limb salvage
  • Ingrown toenails and general nail care

See our complete list of services for the full range of conditions and procedures we offer.

Planning a same-day or urgent visit

If you're dealing with a sudden foot or ankle emergency and are willing to make the drive, call ahead — 801-451-7500 for Farmington or 801-627-2122 for South Ogden — and we'll work to fit you in the same day whenever possible.

Insurance & scheduling

We accept nearly all major insurance carriers, including United Healthcare, Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, PEHP, Tricare, and Aetna. Call our office ahead of your trip and our staff will help verify your out-of-state coverage before you travel.

Common Questions

FAQs for Wyoming patients

Do you have a clinic in Wyoming?

No — we have two clinics in Utah, in Farmington and South Ogden. Both are reachable from southwestern Wyoming via I-80 and I-84, and many of our patients from Evanston, Kemmerer, and the Rock Springs area travel to us for care.

How far is the drive from Evanston or Rock Springs?

From Evanston, both clinics are roughly a 60-90 minute drive via I-80 and I-84. From Kemmerer, Rock Springs, or Green River, plan for about two to three hours depending on the clinic and traffic.

Which clinic should I choose?

Either clinic offers the same physicians and treatment options — choose whichever is a shorter drive for you. Our team can also help you decide when you call to schedule.

Can you see me the same day for an emergency?

We reserve time each day for foot and ankle emergencies. Call ahead — 801-451-7500 for Farmington or 801-627-2122 for South Ogden — and we'll do our best to work you in the same day.

Will my insurance be accepted if I'm from out of state?

We accept nearly all major national insurance carriers, including United Healthcare, Medicare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, PEHP, Tricare, and Aetna. Call ahead of your trip and our team will help verify your specific out-of-state coverage.

Do you offer surgery, or just consultations?

We offer the full range of care, from conservative treatment to advanced surgical procedures like Lapiplasty bunion correction. We'll work with you to plan a schedule that minimizes the number of trips needed for surgery and follow-up.
