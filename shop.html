Shop Braces & Orthotics

The bracing and support products our physicians actually recommend to patients — including an exclusive discount through our ARYSE partnership.

Why we recommend the products we do

We only put our name behind products we'd genuinely recommend to our own patients. Whether you need extra ankle support during recovery, a brace for an active lifestyle, or custom orthotics for everyday comfort, our team can help you find the right fit — in the office or online.

ARYSE ankle bracing — save with code WFAI

Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute has partnered with ARYSE, a leading maker of low-profile ankle braces built for athletes and active patients recovering from sprains, instability, or post-surgical protection. ARYSE braces are designed to fit inside your regular shoe without sacrificing support, which makes them a favorite among our patients with lateral ankle instability or recent ankle injuries.

As a patient of our practice, use code WFAI at checkout on aryse.com for a discount on your order. Not sure which brace is right for you? Ask your provider at your next visit — we're happy to make a recommendation based on your specific injury or activity level.

Custom orthotics

For patients dealing with flat feet, plantar fasciitis, or general arch pain, custom orthotics built specifically for your foot can make a lasting difference. We fit and order these in-office rather than through an online store — learn more on our custom orthotics page or ask about them at your next appointment.

Ordering & fitting

Most bracing and support products are best chosen with input from your podiatrist, since the right option depends on your specific diagnosis and activity goals. For ARYSE products, you can order directly through their website using our discount code. For orthotics and other in-office items, we'll handle fitting and ordering as part of your visit.

Questions about a product?

If you're not sure which product fits your situation, reach out to either office and our team can point you in the right direction — or bring it up at your next appointment.

Common Questions

Shop FAQs

What is ARYSE and why do you recommend it?

ARYSE makes low-profile ankle braces designed to fit inside a normal shoe while still providing meaningful support. We recommend it to patients recovering from ankle sprains, instability, or who want extra protection during sports and activity.

How do I use the WFAI discount code?

Visit aryse.com, choose the brace that fits your needs, and enter code WFAI at checkout to receive your patient discount.

Do you sell orthotics directly in the office?

Yes. Custom orthotics are fitted and ordered in-office as part of an appointment, since they're built specifically to your foot. Learn more on our custom orthotics page.

Can I order products without visiting the clinic?

ARYSE bracing can be ordered directly online at aryse.com using our discount code. Custom orthotics and other fitted items require an in-office visit so we can properly measure and order them for you.

Do you accept insurance for products?

Coverage varies by plan and product. For items fitted in our office, like custom orthotics, our staff can check your specific insurance benefits before you order. For questions about a particular product, give us a call.
