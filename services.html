Our Services

Foot & Ankle Services in Farmington & South Ogden

Everything we treat, from same-day emergencies — ingrown toenails, sprains, breaks — through to reconstructive surgery. Call either clinic and we'll get you seen.

Heel & arch

Pain under the heel, along the arch, or with your first steps of the day.

5
  • Heel Pain Relief for stubborn arch and heel pain so you can get back on your feet.
  • Plantar Fasciitis That stabbing first-step pain has an answer — we'll help you find lasting relief.
  • Calcaneal Fractures Expert care for heel bone breaks, built around getting you back on solid ground.
  • Flat Feet Custom support built for your gait, so fallen arches stop slowing you down.
  • Posterior Tibial Tendon Support and treatment for the tendon that holds up your arch, before it gives way.

Ankle & Achilles

Sprains, breaks, a wobbly ankle, or pain at the back of the heel.

6
  • Ankle Fractures From setting to recovery, we'll help you heal strong and get moving again.
  • Lateral Ankle Instability Rebuild confidence and stability after repeated sprains or a wobbly ankle.
  • Dual Syndesmosis TightRope An advanced fixation option for high ankle sprains that keeps you moving sooner.
  • Talar Dome Lesions Careful diagnosis and treatment for deep ankle joint injuries that limit your stride.
  • Tarsal Tunnel Syndrome Relief for the tingling, numbness, or burning of a pinched nerve near the ankle.
  • Achilles Tendonitis From nagging tightness to tendon pain, minimally-invasive care for a stronger comeback.

Toes, forefoot & midfoot

Bunions, a stiff or aching toe, and breaks in the front half of the foot.

7
  • Bunion Removal Modern correction for straighter alignment, faster recovery, and lasting comfort.
  • Tailor's Bunion Gentle, targeted care for the bump on the outside of your little toe.
  • Lapiplasty A 3D bunion correction that gets to the root of the problem — not just the bump.
  • Hallux Rigidus Treatment for a stiff, aching big toe joint that's making every step harder.
  • Toe & Metatarsal Fractures Careful, comfortable care for breaks in the bones of your toes and midfoot.
  • Fifth Metatarsal Fractures Precise treatment for this common foot break, from diagnosis through recovery.
  • LisFranc Injuries Specialized treatment for these often-missed midfoot injuries, done right the first time.

Arthritis & joint pain

Ongoing joint pain, stiffness, swelling, and sudden flare-ups.

3
  • Arthritis Personalized plans to ease joint pain and keep your feet moving comfortably.
  • Rheumatoid Arthritis Coordinated foot & ankle care for the unique challenges of RA, so you stay mobile.
  • Gout Fast relief for sudden, painful flare-ups, plus a plan to help prevent the next one.

Diabetic foot care

Prevention, wound care, and protecting long-term mobility.

3
  • Diabetic Foot Care Dedicated, proactive care through our sister clinic, Wasatch CAP, protecting what keeps you mobile.
  • Charcot Arthropathy Specialized management to protect fragile joints and keep you steady on your feet.
  • Limb Salvage Advanced, compassionate care focused on preserving mobility and quality of life.

Nails, orthotics & in-office care

Quick in-office treatments and support built for your foot.

3
  • Ingrown Nails Quick, comfortable in-office relief for painful ingrown toenails — same-day available.
  • Custom Orthotics Inserts built for your foot and your lifestyle — supportive without slowing you down.
  • Conscious Sedation A calm, comfortable option for procedures, right here in our office.
