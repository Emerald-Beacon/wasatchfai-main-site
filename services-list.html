Common Questions

Services list FAQs

How is this different from the main Services page?

Our main services page is a searchable, visual grid with short descriptions for each condition. This page is a simple alphabetical index if you already know what you're looking for and just want a fast link.

What if my condition isn't listed here?

This list covers the conditions and procedures we treat most often, but foot and ankle care covers a lot of ground. If you don't see your symptom listed, call the office nearest you and we'll help point you in the right direction.

Do I need a referral to be seen for one of these conditions?

Most patients do not need a referral to see us, though this can depend on your specific insurance plan. Our front desk can confirm your plan's requirements when you call to schedule.

Can I be treated for more than one condition in the same visit?

Often, yes. Many foot and ankle issues are related, and your provider will evaluate everything that's bothering you during your appointment rather than limiting the visit to a single complaint.

Do you treat both feet and ankles, or just one?

We treat the full range of foot and ankle conditions, from toenail issues to ankle fractures and everything in between. Our physicians are board-certified and fellowship-trained specifically in foot and ankle care.
