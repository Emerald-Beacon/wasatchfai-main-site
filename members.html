Patient Portal & Member Resources

Everything you need to manage your care with Wasatch Foot & Ankle Institute — from portal access to appointment requests and billing.

Access your patient portal

Existing patients can log in to view appointment details and communicate with our office. If you don't yet have portal access or need help logging in, give either office a call and our front desk team will get you set up.

Patient Portal Login

Request or manage an appointment

Ready to be seen, or need to reschedule? You can request an appointment online, or call the location nearest you directly. Same-day appointments are held for emergencies such as ingrown toenails, sprains, and fractures.

Pay your bill online

We partner with InstaMed for secure online bill pay. You can view and pay your balance any time at pay.InstaMed.com/WASATCHFOOT without needing to call the office.

New patient forms & insurance

Coming in for your first visit? Give us a call and we'll send new patient intake forms ahead of time so you can fill them out before you arrive, which helps keep your appointment on schedule. Our front desk can also confirm whether your insurance plan is accepted before your visit.

Questions about your account?

Our front desk is your best resource for portal access, billing questions, and appointment changes. Reach out to either location and we'll take care of it.

Common Questions

Patient portal FAQs

How do I access the patient portal?

Use the Patient Portal Login link above, or call the location nearest you if you need your login information or first-time setup help. Our front desk can walk you through it over the phone.

Can I request an appointment online?

Yes. Use our request an appointment page and our team will follow up to confirm a time. For same-day emergencies, calling the office directly is fastest.

How do I pay my bill?

You can pay online any time through InstaMed at pay.InstaMed.com/WASATCHFOOT, or call our office if you'd rather pay over the phone or have a question about your statement.

What if I forgot my portal password?

Call the office nearest you and our front desk team can help reset your access. For your security, password resets are handled by staff rather than automatically online.

Is my information kept secure?

Yes. Your medical and billing information is handled in accordance with HIPAA privacy standards, whether you're communicating with us through the portal, by phone, or in person.
